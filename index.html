Why the name?

Head chef. Every other weekend.

For a lot of dads, Friday pickup means going from “helps with dinner” to “head chef” overnight. No training. Two kids. A fridge with ketchup, half a lime and something that used to be cheese.

We’ve been there. So we made a sauce for it. Pancakes Saturday, tacos Sunday, and somehow it all tastes like you knew what you were doing.

It’s for every dad, on every schedule, and anyone who has ever stared at a pan and hoped. The joke is on the cooking. Never the family.
